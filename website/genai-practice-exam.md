# Practice exam

Practice for the **Databricks Certified Generative AI Engineer Associate** exam with original
questions written against the exam guide that has been live since March 18, 2026.

- **Full mock exam:** 45 questions in 90 minutes, spread across domains like the real exam
  (14 / 14 / 30 / 22 / 8 / 12 %). You see the explanations only after you submit.
- **Domain drill:** every question from one domain, with feedback after each one.
- **Review my mistakes:** questions you got wrong come back until you answer them correctly.

Your results and your mistakes deck are stored **only in this browser**.

!!! info "How to read your score"
    Databricks does not publish a passing score. Aim for **80% or higher on two different
    mocks** before you book the real exam, and look at the domain table: a weak domain with a
    high weight (Application Development, 30%) costs the most points.
